# Account ownership — decisions still required

The application enforces local user ownership of profiles, campaigns, payment orders and records. It does not establish legal ownership of external assets.

Before real-provider activation, document the actual owner and granted permissions for each candidate Page, ad account, application and merchant account. Decide who pays Meta directly, what the platform charges, refund terms and when a service fee is earned. No agency master credit line, guaranteed approval, escrow arrangement, joint creative ownership or fixed refund deadline is assumed by implementation.

Current deposits are recorded as customer credit. Commission settings remain a pricing configuration and are not proof of earned revenue or paid advertising expense. Historical summaries are not recertified as real transactions.
